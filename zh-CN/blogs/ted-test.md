### 00:04

**There is something you know about me, something very personal, and there is something I know about every one of you and that's very central to your concerns. There is something that we know about everyone we meet anywhere in the world, on the street, that is $^{[1]}$*the very mainspring of* whatever they do and whatever they put up with. And that is that all of us want to be happy. In this, we are all together. How we imagine our happiness, that differs from one another, but it's already a lot that we have all in common, that we want to be happy.**

你对我有所了解，是件非常私密的事；而我对你们每个人也了如指掌，这正是你们关切的核心所在。无论在世界的哪个角落，无论在街头巷尾，我们遇见的每个人身上，都存在着某种共同特质——那正是驱动他们所作所为、支撑他们忍受一切的根本动力。那就是：我们都渴望幸福。在这点上，我们同舟共济。虽然对幸福的想象因人而异，但我们共同渴求幸福这件事本身，已然是弥足珍贵的共通点。

### 01:00

**Now my topic is gratefulness. What is the connection between happiness and gratefulness? Many people would say, well, that's very easy. When you are happy, you are grateful. But think again. Is it really the happy people that are grateful? We all know $^{[2]}$*quite a number of* people who have everything that it would take to be happy, and they are not happy, because they want something else or they want more of the same. And we all know people who have lots of $^{[3]}$*misfortune*, misfortune that we ourselves would not want to have, and they are deeply happy. They $^{[4]}$*radiate* happiness. You are surprised. Why? Because they are grateful. So it is not happiness that makes us grateful. It's gratefulness that makes us happy. If you think it's happiness that makes you grateful, think again. It's gratefulness that makes you happy.**

现在我要谈的是感恩。幸福与感恩之间有何关联？许多人会说，这很简单——当你幸福时，自然心怀感恩。但请再思考：当真只有幸福的人才会感恩吗？我们都认识不少拥有一切幸福要素却并不快乐的人，因为他们总渴求其他事物，或渴望更多同类事物。我们同样见过许多遭遇不幸的人——那些我们自己都不愿经历的厄运——却依然深陷幸福之中。他们浑身散发着幸福的光芒，令人惊叹。为什么？因为他们心怀感恩。所以，并非幸福造就感恩，而是感恩带来幸福。若你认为是幸福让你心怀感恩，请再思考一次：正是感恩让你幸福。

### 02:17

**Now, we can ask, what do we really mean by gratefulness? And how does it work? I $^{[5]}$*appeal* to your own experience. We all know from experience how it goes. We experience something that's valuable to us. Something is given to us that's valuable to us. And it's really given. These two things have to come together. It has to be something valuable, and it's a real gift. You haven't bought it. You haven't earned it. You haven't traded it in. You haven't worked for it. It's just given to you. And when these two things come together, something that's really valuable to me and I realize it's freely given, then gratefulness $^{[6]}$*spontaneously* rises in my heart, happiness spontaneously rises in my heart. That's how gratefulness happens.**

现在我们可以问：感恩的真正含义是什么？它是如何运作的？我请诸位回想自身经历。我们都深知其中的道理：当我们获得珍贵之物，当真正有价值的东西被赠予我们——这两者必须同时存在。它必须是珍贵的，且是真实的馈赠。你没有购买它，没有赚取它，没有交换它，也没有为之劳作。它纯粹是馈赠。当这两者结合——某物对我真正珍贵，且我意识到它是无偿给予时，感恩便自然涌上心头，幸福也自然升腾于心。感恩便是如此产生的。

### 03:23

**Now the key to all this is that we cannot only experience this once in a while. We cannot only have grateful experiences. We can be people who live gratefully. Grateful living, that is the thing. And how can we live gratefully? By experiencing, by becoming aware that every moment is a given moment, as we say. It's a gift. You haven't earned it. You haven't $^{[7]}$*brought it about* in any way. You have no way of $^{[8]}$*assuring* that there will be another moment given to you, and yet, that's the most valuable thing that can ever be given to us, this moment, with all the opportunity that it contains. If we didn't have this present moment, we wouldn't have any opportunity to do anything or experience anything, and this moment is a gift. It's a given moment, as we say.**

这一切的关键在于，我们不能仅偶尔体验感恩，不能仅拥有感恩的瞬间。我们应当成为感恩而活的人。感恩的生活，这才是关键。如何才能感恩而活？通过体验，通过觉察到每个当下都是馈赠的时刻——正如我们所言。这是礼物，你并未赢得它，也未以任何方式促成它。你无法确保下一个时刻会降临，然而此刻正是最珍贵的馈赠——它承载着无限可能。若无当下，我们便无从行动，无从体验。此刻即是礼物，正如我们所言，它是上天赐予的珍贵时刻。

### 04:35

**Now, we say the gift within this gift is really the opportunity. What you are really grateful for is the opportunity, not the thing that is given to you, because if that thing were somewhere else and you didn't have the opportunity to enjoy it, to do something with it, you wouldn't be grateful for it. Opportunity is the gift within every gift, and we have this saying, opportunity knocks only once. Well, think again. Every moment is a new gift, over and over again, and if you miss the opportunity of this moment, another moment is given to us, and another moment. We can $^{[9]}$*avail* ourselves of this opportunity, or we can miss it, and if we avail ourselves of the opportunity, it is the key to happiness. $^{[10]}$*Behold* the master key to our happiness in our own hands. Moment by moment, we can be grateful for this gift.**

现在，我们说这份礼物中的真正馈赠其实是机遇。你真正心怀感激的正是这份机遇，而非被赠予的物品本身——因为倘若那件物品在别处，而你没有机会去享受它、去创造价值，你便不会心怀感激。机遇是每份礼物中的珍贵馈赠，世人常言“机会只敲一次门”。然而，请再思之。每个当下都是崭新的馈赠，周而复始。若错失此刻的机遇，新的时刻便会降临，再新的时刻又将到来。我们可以把握机遇，亦可错失良机——而把握机遇正是通往幸福的钥匙。看啊，通往幸福的总钥匙就在我们手中。在每个当下的馈赠中，我们都能心怀感恩。

### 05:45

**Does that mean that we can be grateful for everything? Certainly not. We cannot be grateful for violence, for war, for $^{[11]}$*oppression*, for $^{[12]}$*exploitation*. On the personal level, we cannot be grateful for the loss of a friend, for unfaithfulness, for bereavement. But I didn't say we can be grateful for everything. I said we can be grateful in every given moment for the opportunity, and even when we are $^{[13]}$*confronted* with something that is terribly difficult, we can rise to this $^{[14]}$*occasion* and respond to the opportunity that is given to us. It isn't as bad as it might seem. Actually, when you look at it and experience it, you find that most of the time, what is given to us is the opportunity to enjoy, and we only miss it because we are rushing through life and we are not stopping to see the opportunity.**

这是否意味着我们可以对一切心怀感激？当然不是。我们无法对暴力、战争、压迫和剥削心怀感激。在个人层面，我们无法对失去挚友、遭遇背叛或承受丧亲之痛心怀感激。但我从未说过我们可以对一切心怀感激。我所说的是：在每个当下，我们都能为机遇心怀感激。即便遭遇极度艰难的处境，我们也能迎难而上，把握所赐予的机遇。事情往往没有表面看起来那么糟。事实上，当你真正去观察和体验时，会发现多数时候我们获得的都是享受的契机，而我们之所以错失，只因匆匆忙忙地穿行于人生，从未驻足凝视这些机遇。

### 06:53

**But once in a while, something very difficult is given to us, and when this difficult thing occurs to us, it's a challenge to rise to that opportunity, and we can $^{[15]}$*rise to* it by learning something which is sometimes painful. Learning patience, for $^{[16]}$*instance*. We have been told that the road to peace is not a $^{[17]}$*sprint*, but is more like a marathon. That takes patience. That's difficult. It may be to stand up for your opinion, to stand up for your conviction. That's an opportunity that is given to us. To learn, to suffer, to stand up, all these opportunities are given to us, but they are opportunities, and those who avail themselves of those opportunities are the ones that we admire. They make something out of life. And those who fail get another opportunity. We always get another opportunity. That's the wonderful richness of life.**

但偶尔，命运会将极大的挑战赋予我们。当困境降临，如何把握机遇便成为考验——我们需通过学习某些痛苦的功课来应对。比如学会耐心。人们常说通往和平的道路并非短跑，更像是马拉松。这需要耐心，而耐心难得。或许是捍卫自己的观点，坚守自己的信念。这正是命运赋予我们的机遇。学习、承受、挺身而出——所有这些机遇都向我们敞开，但它们终究是机遇。那些把握机遇之人值得我们钦佩，他们让生命绽放光彩。而失败者终将迎来新的机会。我们永远拥有新的机会，这正是生命的奇妙丰饶之处。

### 08:01

**So how can we find a method that will $^{[18]}$*harness* this? How can each one of us find a method for living gratefully, not just once in a while being grateful, but moment by moment to be grateful. How can we do it? It's a very simple method. It's so simple that it's actually what we were told as children when we learned to cross the street. Stop. Look. Go. That's all. But how often do we stop? We rush through life. We don't stop. We miss the opportunity because we don't stop. We have to stop. We have to get quiet. And we have to build stop signs into our lives.**

那么，我们该如何找到一种方法来驾驭这种力量？我们每个人又该如何找到一种方法，让感恩成为生活的常态——不仅是偶尔心怀感激，而是时刻心怀感恩。我们该如何做到？方法其实极其简单。简单到它正是儿时过马路时被教导的规则：停。看。走。仅此而已。但我们停下脚步的次数又有多少？我们匆匆忙忙地穿行于人生，从不停驻。正因停不下，才错失了无数机会。我们必须停下，必须静心，必须在生命中竖起停驻的标识。

### 08:55

**When I was in Africa some years ago and then came back, I noticed water. In Africa where I was, I didn't have drinkable water. Every time I turned on the $^{[19]}$*faucet*, I was overwhelmed. Every time I clicked on the light, I was so grateful. It made me so happy. But after a while, this $^{[20]}$*wears off*. So I put little stickers on the light switch and on the water faucet, and every time I turned it on, water. So leave it up to your own imagination. You can find whatever works best for you, but you need stop signs in your life. And when you stop, then the next thing is to look. You look. You open your eyes. You open your ears. You open your nose. You open all your senses for this wonderful richness that is given to us. There is no end to it, and that is what life is all about, to enjoy, to enjoy what is given to us.**

几年前我在非洲时，回国后便格外留意水。在非洲待的地方，我没有饮用水。每次打开水龙头时，我都感到无比震撼。每次按下电灯开关，我都心怀感激。这让我无比欣喜。但这种感动很快消退。于是我在开关和水龙头贴上小贴纸，每次使用时便提醒自己：水，就在眼前。具体方式可随心而定，但生活需要这样的“停车标志”。停下脚步后，下一步便是凝望。你凝望。睁开双眼。竖起双耳。张开鼻腔。调动所有感官去感受这馈赠于我们的丰饶奇观。它永无止境，而人生的真谛正在于此——享受，享受上天赐予的一切。

### 12:15

**And it doesn't make for equality, but it makes for equal respect, and that is the important thing. The future of the world will be a network, not a pyramid turned upside down. The revolution of which I am speaking is a nonviolent revolution, and it's so revolutionary that it even revolutionizes the very concept of a revolution, because the normal revolution is one where the power pyramid is turned upside down and those who were on the bottom are now on the top and are doing exactly the same thing that the ones before. What we need is a networking of smaller groups, smaller and smaller groups who know one another, who interact with one another, and that is a grateful world.**

这并非追求平等，而是追求平等的尊重，这才是关键所在。世界的未来将是网络化，而非倒置的金字塔。我所说的革命是非暴力的革命，其革命性之强甚至颠覆了革命的概念本身——因为常规革命不过是权力金字塔倒转，原先底层者登上顶端后，却重蹈前任覆辙。我们需要的是由越来越小的群体构成的网络——彼此相识、相互协作的小群体，这才是充满感恩的世界。
